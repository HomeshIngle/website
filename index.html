How FoodRescue Works

Three simple steps to rescue food and feed someone in need.

01
📋

List Surplus Food

Restaurants, cafes and hostels list their surplus food on the platform in just a few taps — quantity, type, and pickup time.

→
02
📍

Location-Based Match

Our platform uses Google Maps to instantly match donors with nearby NGOs and volunteers who can collect the food.

→
03
🔔

Real-Time Pickup

NGOs and volunteers get real-time notifications and collect the food before it goes to waste — fast and simple.

📊
Impact Dashboard

See how many meals you've rescued and your environmental impact in real time.

Platform Features

Everything You Need

Built with React.js, Node.js, MySQL & Google Maps API for a seamless experience.

🗺️

Location-Based Matching

Google Maps integration finds the nearest NGOs and volunteers to your surplus food instantly.

🔔

Real-Time Notifications

Instant alerts ensure food is collected before it's wasted — no delays, no missed opportunities.

📊

Impact Dashboard

Track meals rescued, CO₂ saved, and your contribution to the community with beautiful metrics.

🤝

NGO & Volunteer Network

Connect directly with verified NGOs and volunteers across Pune ready to collect and distribute.

📱

Web & Mobile

Works on any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile. No special app required to get started.

🛡️

Food Safety Guidance

Built-in food safety and hygiene guidelines so donors and receivers handle food responsibly.
